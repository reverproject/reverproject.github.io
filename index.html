Objective

REVER aims to study and develop a reversible system for small-scale Waste Heat Recovery (WHR) applications. The reversibility consists in the system's ability to operate alternatively as an Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) and a High-Temperature Heat Pump (HTHP), where the former cycle utilizes the heat wasted in industrial processes to produce electrical energy, while the latter upgrades it by raising its temperature level.

Different configurations of the cycles and different types of Reversible Flow Machines (RFMs) functioning as both expanders and compressors will be analysed. The most promising solution will be tested experimentally at the research facility of eCampus University.

contact

If you wish to contact us, please send an e-mail to the project coordinator, Professor Luca Cioccolanti, at eCampus University: luca.cioccolanti@uniecampus.it

REVER is a research and innovation project with the objective of enhancing REVersible Energy Recovery systems to promote their application at the small-scale and in the industrial sector.

The project is funded by the Ministry of University and Research (MUR) of the Italian government, and participated by research groups and faculty members of three institutions: eCampus University, the University of Pisa, and the Free University of Bozen.
